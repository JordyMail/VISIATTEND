--- PARAGRAF 1: PRIVACY ---

To address the privacy constraint outlined in Aspect 1, VISIATTEND does not store
raw facial photographs at any point in the system. During registration, facial
images are processed immediately on the server to extract a 512-dimensional
numerical embedding vector using the ArcFace model. Only this encrypted embedding
vector is persisted in the database. Since the original face photograph cannot be
reconstructed from an embedding vector alone, this approach satisfies the principle
of privacy by design and significantly reduces legal risk under personal data
protection regulations.

--- PARAGRAF 2: GAMIFICATION ---

In terms of gamification, VISIATTEND implements a point-based leaderboard system
where users accumulate points based on three attendance metrics: total attendance
count, punctuality (on-time check-in before the session deadline), and attendance
consistency streaks (consecutive sessions attended without absence). The leaderboard
is visible to all users within the same group or class, displaying rankings by
accumulated points. To address ethical concerns, absences tied to approved leave are
excluded from penalty calculation, ensuring the leaderboard promotes positive
engagement rather than punitive comparison.
